What is Dance?

More Than Just Movement

Dance is a form of artistic expression that uses body movements, rhythm and music to communicate emotions, stories and ideas.

Across the world, different cultures have developed their own unique dance traditions. From classical Indian dances to energetic street dances, every form has its own history and identity.
